
# 🚒 AI-Powered Emergency Response Decision Support System

### Software Engineering Capstone Project | 2026

**Research Laboratory:** DISP – Université Lumière Lyon 2, France  
**Operational Partner:** SDIS 42 – French Fire and Rescue Service  
**Domain:** Artificial Intelligence · Emergency Management · Decision Support

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as part of my final-year Software Engineering capstone, in collaboration with the DISP Research Laboratory and French firefighters (SDIS 42).

The objective was to design an **AI-powered decision-support system** to assist emergency responders in anticipating operational risks, analyzing incident situations, and preparing incident action plans.

By combining **Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Python**, the project explored how Generative AI can support complex operational decision-making in emergency situations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Support operational risk anticipation during emergency incidents.
- Assist fire officers in analyzing complex intervention scenarios.
- Generate context-aware decision-support information using LLMs.
- Improve access to relevant operational information through RAG.
- Transform operational requirements into an AI-powered application.
- Design a solution that considers data confidentiality and GDPR requirements.
